FTP HARDENING

FTP is a legacy protocol that commonly sends credentials without transport encryption. Anonymous access must be intentional and confined to non-sensitive data.

LEARNING GOAL

Identify the misconfiguration in the synthetic evidence and explain a defensive correction.
